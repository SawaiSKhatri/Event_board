General Inquiries

How can I submit an event to the board?

You can propose a new event by navigating to our Submit Event portal, filling out the required date and location fields, and submitting the form.

What types of campus events are accepted?

We welcome technical workshops, coding hackathons, academic seminars, AI/ML study groups, and cultural club activities hosted at IIIT Vadodara.
